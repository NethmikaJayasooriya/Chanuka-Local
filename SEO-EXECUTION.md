## Implemented in source

- Home page: visible single H1, local service summary, qualifications, LKR starting prices, dated Google review proof, service links and Sinhala summary.
- Core CV/LinkedIn pages: direct service answers, career-level price cards, relevant local/overseas application context, clearly labelled fictional writing examples, Sinhala summaries, practical FAQs and internal links.
- New LinkedIn guide: target-role worksheet, headline examples, About structure, CV consistency checks, measurement advice, official LinkedIn sources, and a keyboard-accessible checklist. No login, personal data collection, ranking score or stored selections. Editorial attribution does not claim that Chanuka personally reviewed this new draft.
- Google reviews: updated central source to 121 and the user-supplied profile URL. Removed unsupported callback-time statistics, paid-client verification claims and self-serving aggregate-rating markup.
- Entity graph: Organization, Person, WebSite, WebPage, Service, actual tier-priced Offers, Article/ProfilePage and breadcrumbs where relevant. No made-up office address. Unpriced services do not inherit invented price ranges.
- Canonical URLs and sitemap hosts: use `https://www.chanukajeewantha.lk`, the final host observed after apex redirects. The reciprocal .com alternate was adjusted locally to match.
- Crawl/index: preserve public crawler access; noindex private workflows; preview deployments receive noindex headers and restrictive robots. Crawl access alone does not guarantee AI citation.
- Sitemaps: use content-specific update dates, escape XML, deduplicate per file, omit unavailable dates and exclude unsupported CMS route types/invalid slugs and static/CMS article collisions.
- Redirects: canonicalize LinkedIn spelling and pricing aliases; add relevant known legacy blog/guide mappings; route global country URLs to actual .com destinations rather than missing .lk country routes. This is not a complete migration of every historical URL.
- AI reference files: align service summaries, tier definitions, dated reviews and file-delivery scope with visible content. `llms.txt` is an optional reference, not a Google ranking requirement.
- Conversion/trust: add visible WhatsApp to Contact, local email subject, Search Console verification-token support, accurate cookie consent copy.
- Performance: render static hero on the server, reduce optimized hero image quality from 95 to 75, and remove delayed entrance opacity from inner-page H1/lead copy so important text appears immediately. This is an optimization, not a measured Core Web Vitals pass.
- Previous admin task: removed stale middleware redirect that prevented access to the separately scoped .lk admin. Authorization still runs in the existing guard and server actions.
